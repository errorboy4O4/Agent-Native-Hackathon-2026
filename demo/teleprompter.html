DataPilot demo, about 90 seconds Space or → next · ← back · + / − text size

Before you press record

  • Run pnpm dev in D:\AgentNativeHackathon\datapilot, then open http://localhost:8080/datapilot.
  • Click Open a different file so the intake screen (“Drop in a CSV and ask it questions”) is showing.
  • Close other tabs. Never open .env on camera. Browser window about 1280 px wide.
  • Put this page on a second screen or beside the recorder. Say the big text; do the blue action.

0:00 – 0:08 · Intake screen showing

Do: nothing yet. Look at the camera, then at the screen.

“This is DataPilot. You drop in a CSV, and it answers questions about it in plain English.”

0:08 – 0:15

Do: click Online shop sales.

“No code and no spreadsheet. I’ll use a built-in sample of shop orders.”

0:15 – 0:30 · Profile and charts appear

Do: let the page load, then slowly scroll to the charts.

“It has read every row: two hundred orders, six columns, nothing missing. These charts were drawn by the same actions the AI agent uses.”

0:30 – 0:42

Do: on the Most common product chart, open its dropdown and pick country.

“I stay in control. I can point any chart at a different column, and it updates straight away.”

0:42 – 0:52

Do: point at the price against quantity chart and its correlation line.

“For two number columns it shows the correlation. Every number here is computed in code, not guessed by a model.”

0:52 – 1:02

Do: scroll up to Ask about this data. Type Which category sells the most units? and press Ask.

“Now I’ll ask it a question, the way I’d ask a colleague.”

If it takes a few seconds, keep talking: “It’s working out the answer now.” Tip: ask once before recording so a first answer is already on screen.

1:02 – 1:15 · Answer and numbers appear

Do: point at the answer, then at Where the numbers come from.

“Clothing, 281 units. The model didn’t calculate that. It asked for an exact total, and here is the table it used. If it ever states a number it can’t back up, the app flags it.”

1:15 – 1:22

Do: click Pin this answer, then scroll down to Pinned.

“I can pin an answer, and it keeps the numbers behind it.”

1:22 – 1:30 · Scroll to the privacy line at the bottom

Do: scroll to the footer sentence and hold for two seconds.

“It’s built with Agent-Native. And the model never sees your whole file, only summary statistics and the results of exact calculations. That’s DataPilot.”

Optional extra, 15 seconds · only if you are under 90 seconds

Do: click Open a different file and choose Messy sign-ups.

“It copes with messy data too: blanks, and a stray letter in a number column, which it flags.”

After recording: open the Clips link in a private window to check it plays without logging in.
